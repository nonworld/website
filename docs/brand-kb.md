# NON brand knowledge base — approved

Compiled from public interviews and articles (Onya Magazine, Dry Atlas,
Clean Slate Clinic podcast, My Daily Business podcast, New Rules Bev).
Everything below is paraphrased, not quoted, from those sources.
Reviewed and approved by Aaron, cleared for Somm to state as fact.

## Origin story
- The idea began in London in November 2017, eating through Michelin-starred
  fine-dining tasting menus.
- At several restaurants, bartenders built alcohol-free pairings that
  were more inventive than the wine pairing being served alongside
  them.
- One pairing at The Clove Club in London was the specific moment it
  clicked, these drinks were genuinely good, but only existed inside
  that one room, for that one night.
- Aaron felt it was a waste that these drinks only ever lived and died
  inside fine-dining restaurants, never bottled, never available
  outside that context.
- Aaron's background is in cosmetics chemistry, not drinks or
  hospitality, before founding NON.
- NON launched in 2019.

## Naming and positioning
- The name "NON" was chosen to be bold and unapologetic, not
  apologetic or diminished the way "non-alcoholic" often sounds.
- The name is also meant to reflect the company's culture more broadly,
  not fitting neatly into an existing category or convention.
- NON is explicitly positioned as not trying to imitate wine varietals.
  It isn't "a fake rosé" or "a fake red", it's its own category built
  from fruit, tannin, salinity and acidity.

## Flavour philosophy
- Aaron approaches flavour like a chef, not like a winemaker: the same
  four levers (fruit, tannin, salinity, acidity) get used in every
  bottle, achieved through real technique, not flavouring.
- Examples of that technique: roasting pears until natural sugars
  caramelise, adding olive brine for salinity; dehydrating oranges over
  48 hours and combining with a specific salt for another bottle.
- The goal is that every ingredient named on the label is actually
  tasteable in the glass, not just listed.
- Deliberately avoids the two failure modes Aaron sees in the category:
  too sweet (reads as a children's drink) or too dry/bitter (trying too
  hard to mimic alcohol's edge). NON aims for its own balance instead
  of either extreme.

## Market position
- Aaron describes Australia as ahead of most other markets in
  non-alcoholic drinking culture.
- He encourages restaurants to build a dedicated non-alcoholic section
  on their menu rather than treating it as an afterthought, citing that
  a meaningful share of diners now choose non-alcoholic options.
- NON has picked up international recognition, including a Gold at the
  Sommeliers Choice Awards, part of a run of accolades across a short
  period.

## Founder voice / recurring lines
- Aaron's own description of entrepreneurship: a willingness to start
  before everything is figured out, learning as you go rather than
  waiting for certainty.
- On rejection: being told no by one person isn't a reason to stop,
  and if it is, the underlying business idea probably wasn't a fit to
  begin with.
