About

We don't mimic wine. We make something else.

A chef-led drinks company in Melbourne, building wine alternatives out of real produce — fruit, tannin, salinity, acidity — and nothing that was ever fermented.

The origin
A drinks pairing in London that never left the restaurant.

In 2017 Aaron Trotman was eating through Michelin-starred rooms in London. He took the alcohol pairing; the non-drinker at the table was handed something the bartenders had built by hand — considered, complex, matched to the plate — and it existed nowhere outside that dining room.

He had no background in drinks. He came from cosmetics chemistry, which turned out to matter more than a wine list would have. NON launched in 2019 as the thing that should have been in a bottle already.

“I thought it was a real shame that these drinks lived and died in fancy restaurants.”
Aaron Trotman, founder
What we actually do
Every glass is built from four things.
01
Fruit

Cold-steeped, dehydrated, roasted or reduced — whichever method gets the most out of it. Rarely juiced.

02
Tannin

Chamomile, cinnamon, tea, hops, citrus pith. Grip on the finish, so it holds a plate instead of washing through.

03
Salinity

Murray River salt, kombu. Salt makes food taste more like itself — a lever wine never had.

04
Acidity

Barossa verjus, citrus, vinegar. Resets the palate between bites — and cools chilli instead of stoking it.

The difficult part
Water doesn't carry flavour the way alcohol does.

Alcohol is a solvent. It grabs aroma compounds, holds them, and releases them in the glass. Take it away and you lose the vehicle, not just the buzz — which is why most zero-proof drinks fall back on sugar.

So every ingredient is processed on its own before anything is blended: cooked, steeped, sous-vide, roasted, dehydrated. It's slow, and it's the entire product.

Who makes it
Chefs, food scientists, bartenders and winemakers — in-house.

What we're not
Not de-alcoholised

No wine is made and then stripped. Nothing here has ever been fermented.

Not a mocktail

Mocktails are mixed to order and usually sweet. This is cooked in a facility and bottled dry.

Not an imitation

It takes the seat wine had at the table. It doesn't try to taste like the thing that sat there.
