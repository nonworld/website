Why any of this works without alcohol
Tannin

From tea, chamomile, cinnamon and hops — not grape skins. It is what makes a drink grip fat instead of sliding off it.

Salinity

Murray River salt, 100–140mg a serve. Salt makes food taste more like itself; wine never had this lever.

Acid

Barossa verjus and citrus. It resets the palate between bites — and unlike alcohol, it cools chilli instead of stoking it.
